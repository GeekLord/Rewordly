## Platform

desktop: one Avalonia app for Windows, macOS and Linux (native windows, code-only UI)

## Users

People who write all day in other apps (email, chat, documents, browsers) and want better wording without leaving the app they are in. Multilingual users, including Hindi speakers (inferred from the Hindi menu labels).

## Product Purpose

Select text anywhere, press the copy shortcut twice, and a menu at the pointer rewrites it in a style, proofreads, summarises, explains, translates, chats about it, or reads it aloud. The result window lets the user edit, then paste it back in place. Success: the round trip feels faster than retyping.

## Positioning

Works inside every app on all three desktop systems, not in a browser tab. Requests go straight from the user's machine to the AI provider they pick (Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, local Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible server); no Rewordly cloud. Keys live in the OS keychain.

## Brand commitments

- Name: Rewordly. The current app icon is temporary and is NOT a brand reference (owner, 2026-10-25).
- No palette, logo or typeface is fixed yet; the visual world is open.
- Licence: MIT. Third-party icons must be credited.

## Accessibility

Text contrast at least 4.5:1 for body text in light and dark themes; both themes are first-class (System/Light/Dark setting).
